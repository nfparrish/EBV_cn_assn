## 1. Executive Summary & Core Architecture of the Story

Lymphoblastoid cell lines (LCLs) from the 1000 Genomes Project (1kGP) were generated by transforming human peripheral B cells with the **B95-8** reference strain of Epstein-Barr virus (EBV; ` ~78%` pure B95-8, `~22%` carrying endogenous wild-type donor EBV or co-infection across the full 3,200-sample pedigree cohort). Across 1kGP LCLs, EBV episome copy number (`CN`) spans more than two orders of magnitude (`2.8` to `596` copies/cell). This project systematically dissected what `EBV CN` measures, what genetic factors (viral vs. host) control it, how it perturbs the human transcriptome, and how the genetic architecture of LCL viral load compares with population biobank blood read-positivity GWAS (`UK Biobank` and `All of Us`).

### The Eight Core Pillars of Findings

1. **Multi-Platform EBV Copy Number Stability & Zero Narrow-Sense Host Heritability ($h^2 \approx 0$)** ([Figure 1](file:///Users/nfp8/Downloads/EBV_Host_Viral_Genetics_Handoff_2026_10_22/figures/fig1_wgs_architecture_and_heritability.png)):
   - Across `n = 2,449` clean B95-8 1kGP lines (and `3,200 / 3,202` total lines including trios and wild-type carriers), 30x Illumina WGS `log2(CN)` is highly concordant with ~2023 Oxford Nanopore (`ONT`) long-read `log2(CN)` (**$r = +0.9072, n = 890$**) and Phase 3 low-coverage WGS (**$r = +0.8427, n = 1,954$**), with 3-indicator latent reliability of **95.65%** for 30x WGS.
   - In `n = 570` clean 1kGP trios (`565` unique spouse pairs), after demeaning by the 26 populations (`ped3202.txt` column `f[5]`), midparent–offspring correlation ($r_{\text{mid,within}} = +0.2427$) and single-parent–offspring correlation ($r_{\text{sp,within}} = +0.1865$) are indistinguishable from the genetically unrelated **spouse–spouse correlation ($r_{\text{pp,within}} = +0.1815$)**, yielding **narrow-sense host heritability $h^2_{\text{within}} = 2(r_{\text{sp}} - r_{\text{pp}}) = +0.0101 \approx 0$** (and `+0.0109` after conditioning on all viral genetic factors), with a shared family/collection batch component $c^2_{\text{family|pop}} = 0.1815$.
   - Private Cohort Panel-of-Normals (`PoN`)-cleaned WGS `IGH` BCR clonality (`n = 2,313` lines) recovers `92.5%` (`259/280`) of top WGS clones in MAGE RNA-seq (`100.0%` when `>30` WGS reads) and is independent of `log2(CN)` ($\rho = +0.0778, R^2 = 0.6\%$).

2. **Decisive H1 Resolution — EBV DNA Copy Number Tracks Productive Lytic Reactivation, Not Latent Episome Dosage** ([Figure 2](file:///Users/nfp8/Downloads/EBV_Host_Viral_Genetics_Handoff_2026_10_22/figures/fig2_lytic_vs_latent_rna_h1.png)):
   - Across all **779 / 779 MAGE RNA-seq libraries** (`n = 717` unique clean B95-8 donors across 26 populations and 17 sequencing batches, using competitive GENCODE v45 decoy filtering and exact splice-junction counting), **latent Pol II mRNA (`EBNA-1..3C`, `LMP-1`, `LMP-2A/B`) is completely uncoupled from WGS `log2(CN)` ($r = -0.0287$; within-pop $r = -0.0200$; within-pop+batch $r = +0.0454$)**.
   - By contrast, **spliced `BZLF1` (Zta) junction RPM ($r = +0.6477, P = 1.6 \times 10^{-86}$; within-pop+batch $r = +0.6439$)** and all three lytic kinetic classes (`Immediate-Early` $r = +0.6404$, `Early` $r = +0.6244$, `Late` $r = +0.6267$) strongly drive `log2(CN)` and show high cross-thaw biological stability across `n = 24` multi-replicate donors (`70` replicate pairs; cross-batch `BGLF5` $r_{\text{bio}} = +0.950$, `log2(IE/Latent)` $r_{\text{bio}} = +0.910$).
   - High-CN WGS BAMs exhibit physical replication-fork enrichment around the lytic origin **`oriLyt` (`41–55 kb`) vs. the antipode (`110–130 kb`)** (`1.1659` in top-30 high-CN vs. `1.0805` in bottom-30 low-CN).

3. **Aim 1 — Dissection of All 84 Viral Genes** ([Figure 3](file:///Users/nfp8/Downloads/EBV_Host_Viral_Genetics_Handoff_2026_10_22/figures/fig3_viral_gene_dissection_84.png)):
   - Ranking all 84 non-overlapping EBV coding windows (`viral_gene_dissection_84.tsv`) identifies the viral host-shutoff alkaline exonuclease **`BGLF5`** as the #1 transcript predicting `log2(CN)` ($r = +0.6886$, within-pop+batch $r_{\text{wb}} = +0.6694$), followed by `BKRF4` ($+0.6865$), `BKRF3` ($+0.6854$), helicase-primase `BBLF2_BBLF3` ($+0.6846$), and protein kinase `BGLF4` ($+0.6815$).
   - Conditioning on `Spliced BZLF1` ($R^2 = 41.5\%$), lytic DNA replication fork enzymes (`BALF5` polymerase $r_{\text{part}} = +0.244, t = +6.72$; `BBLF2_BBLF3` $r_{\text{part}} = +0.258, t = +7.14$) and late capsid genes (`BcLF1` $r_{\text{part}} = +0.278, t = +7.72$) contribute significant independent prediction—proving **full productive lytic DNA synthesis** rather than abortive immediate-early transcription.

4. **Aim 2 — Viral Genetic Determinants of Copy Number (`IR1` Repeat Expansion, `12,129:T>A`, and *EBNA-2* `p.Ser89Pro`)** ([Figure 4](file:///Users/nfp8/Downloads/EBV_Host_Viral_Genetics_Handoff_2026_10_22/figures/fig4_viral_genetics_ir1_qtl_and_ase.png)):

   - **Sequence CN-QTLs vs. Neutral Stock Barcodes**:
     - The `IBS`-enriched `91,192:G>A` (`BRLF1` `p.S568S`) and `GBR`-enriched `132,965:G>GC` (`BXRF1`) variants are **completely null within populations** ($r_{\text{wp}} = -0.0410$ and $-0.0416$) and show null within-host heteroplasmic RNA vs. DNA allele-specific expression (`91,192:G>A` RNA VAF `19.1%` vs. DNA VAF `17.7%`, $P = 0.082$), proving they are neutral transformation-stock passenger barcodes.
     - Conversely, **`12,129:T>A`** (`128 bp` inside Copy 0 of the `IR1` repeat / `W2–W1` intron) is the **#1 sequence CN-QTL** ($r_{\text{wp}} = +0.1695, P = 2.9 \times 10^{-17}$ in `n = 2,449`; out-of-sample replication in `n = 1,732` non-MAGE holdout lines $r = +0.1736, P = 3.5 \times 10^{-13}$) and increases MAGE `Lytic IE` RNA ($r_{\text{wb}} = +0.1048, P = 0.0049$).
     - Among single-copy wild-type polymorphisms outside `IR1`, ***EBNA-2* `36,480:T>C` (`p.Ser89Pro`, $r = +0.0509, P = 0.0118$)** and ***EBNA-2* `36,494:G>C` (`p.Pro93=`, $r = +0.0571, P = 0.0047$)** sit directly in the **N-terminal Poly-Proline Repeat (`PPR`, `aa 59–98`)** of **EBNA-2**, where `36,480:T>C` (`TCA` $\to$ `CCA`) eliminates the single serine interruption (`Ser89`) inside the 36-residue poly-proline tract (`aa 63–98`) of the **EBNA-2 / EBNA-LP (`IR1`)** Latency III pioneer complex.

5. **Aim 3 — Viral Transcription Mediates 68.5% of the Human Transcriptome & Predicts Between-Population Differential Expression** ([Figure 5](file:///Users/nfp8/Downloads/EBV_Host_Viral_Genetics_Handoff_2026_10_22/figures/fig5_host_mediation_and_bipartite_map.png) & [Figure 6](file:///Users/nfp8/Downloads/EBV_Host_Viral_Genetics_Handoff_2026_10_22/figures/fig6_population_vector_alignment_ibs_gbr.png)):

   - In formal 1-df Baron–Kenny mediation, conditioning `2019 WGS log2(CN)` on **`Spliced BZLF1` eliminates 92.4% (`5,625 / 6,087`) of `WGS CN` host-gene associations** (mediating `86.0%` of directional $R^2$), whereas `78.7%` (`7,732 / 9,819`) of `Spliced BZLF1` hits survive conditioning on `WGS CN`.
   - Using the exogenous DNA **`IR1` repeat count** as a viral genetic instrument identifies **663 human genes at $q < 0.05$** (532 mediated via viral copy number/lytic reactivation, enriched in the **ATR / USP7 / SMC6 / PML-body** episome-maintenance axis) whose effect vector aligns with `WGS CN` at **$r = +0.7636$**.
   - Training each gene's within-population viral slope $\hat{\beta}_{\text{CN}}$ strictly inside the **24 non-IBS, non-GBR populations** (zero sample overlap) predicts observed between-population differential expression ($\overline{Y}_{\text{IBS}} - \overline{Y}_{\text{GBR}}$) between Spanish (`IBS`, median CN `~96`) and British (`GBR`, median CN `~28`) LCLs at **$r = +0.7161$ ($R^2 = 51.3\%$)** across all 20,154 human genes and **$r = +0.8493$ ($R^2 = 72.1\%$)** across the 6,135 CN-sensitive genes.

## 4. Summary of the 10-Point Forensic Code Audit (Fixed in Commit `13a185f`)

During Phase 6, we conducted an exhaustive line-by-line forensic code audit of all scripts used to draw our inferences (`heritability.py`, `viral_genetics_qtl.py`, `host_mediation_bipartite.py`, `prepare_host_gwas_phenotypes.py`, `run_host_gwas_chr.py`, and `run_biobank_lcl_comparison.py`). Core scripts (`heritability.py`, `viral_genetics_qtl.py`, `host_mediation_bipartite.py`, `prepare_host_gwas_phenotypes.py`, `run_host_gwas_chr.py`) passed verification with zero mathematical defects. In `run_biobank_lcl_comparison.py`, we identified and repaired **6 methodological bugs**:

1. **Bug 1 (Major — Corrected)**: `run_biobank_lcl_comparison.py` had computed signed SNP concordance across all `5,409,575` raw unpruned SNPs (violating binomial independence via LD and weighting the MHC/HLA region by `>25,000` correlated SNPs) and tested block-level signed agreement using `mean(Z)` across `~1.7 Mb` blocks (which cancels opposing alleles when LD phase flips). **Fix**: Added **100-kb LD-pruned lead-SNP selection (`30,746` quasi-independent SNPs)** and **lead-SNP-at-max-|Z| block sign testing (`1,636` blocks)**. All 4 tests (`CN_cond`, `BZLF1_cond`, `IR1_cond`, `CN_base`) are 50.0% coin-flip noise ($P = 0.20\text{–}0.92$), confirming that LCL viral copy number does not share polygenic directionality with Biobank blood read positivity.
2. **Bug 2 (Major — Corrected)**: Biobank `UKB_chrEBV.tsv.gz` and `AOU_chrEBV.tsv.gz` per-base pileups were aligned to `EBV_masked` where `IR1` (`13,089–34,320`) and `IR2` (`38,176–40,567`) had `TOTAL = 0` hard-masked coverage (`23 / 112` 1kGP viral sites), which had been silently plotted at `AF = 1e-4` (`-4.0` on log10 scale) and included in the Pearson $r$ denominator (`r = +0.514`). **Fix**: Explicitly flagged those 23 sites as `MASKED_REPEAT_ZERO_COV` and restricted correlation to the **89 unmasked sites (`100%` present in both UKB and AoU; UKB $r = +0.579, P = 2.8 \times 10^{-9}$; AoU $r = +0.625, P = 6.9 \times 10^{-11}$)**.
3. **Bug 3 (Moderate — Corrected)**: The 45-locus lookup used a fixed `±25 kb` window without choosing the SNP closest to the Biobank lead coordinate (`BP_38`) when multiple 1kGP SNPs matched the `P_biobank` ceiling (`1e-300` in HLA). **Fix**: Selected the exact coordinate-nearest SNP ($\min |\text{POS} - \text{BP}_{38}|$) and added `DIST_BP` to `biobank_ebv_loci_comparison.tsv`.
4. **Bug 4 (Moderate — Corrected)**: Unsigned block-maxima correlation (`1,636` LD blocks) did not partial out `log10(n_snps)` per block (`n_snps` correlates with block maximum $|Z|$ at $\rho \approx +0.29$). **Fix**: Added partial Spearman $\rho$ controlling for `[log10(n_snps), is_hla]` (`CN_cond` $\rho_{\text{partial}} = -0.0080, P = 0.747$; `BZLF1_cond` $\rho_{\text{partial}} = -0.0329, P = 0.184$).
5. **Bug 5 (Minor — Corrected)**: `norm.ppf(1 - p/2)` underflowed to `inf` when `P < 1e-300` in HLA. **Fix**: Used `- stats.norm.isf(max(p, 1e-300) / 2.0)`.
6. **Bug 6 (Minor — Corrected)**: Replaced the hard-coded 17-gene subset with a full transcriptome-wide check across all `20,154` MAGE genes and all `43` non-HLA Biobank candidate genes (`36` expressed in LCLs).
