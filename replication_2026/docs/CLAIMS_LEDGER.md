# Claims ledger — shared source of truth

Every stated finding in this project, with the four fields that this session's errors came from
omitting. **A claim is not citable until all four columns are filled.**

- **n / denominator** — the unit the number is computed over. Sites vs calls vs samples vs genes are
  different denominators and have given different answers for the same claim.
- **Input complete?** — was the underlying job finished *when the number was computed*. "Computed" and
  "computed on complete data" are different states and were repeatedly conflated.
- **Control** — and specifically whether the control was *capable of failing*. A control that cannot
  fail validates plumbing, not the assay.
- **Status** — live, superseded, or withdrawn.

## Rule

**When a job completes, every claim derived from its partial output is marked STALE and recomputed
before being cited again.** Three of the prior session's errors were partial-data claims that were never
revisited, and five "settled" claims contained silent script bugs (column off-by-one in `ped3202.txt`,
joining WGS tables against the 731-sample MAGE metadata instead of `ped3202.txt`, and omitting a
cohort CDR3 panel-of-normals on gDNA TRUST4 calls). All were audited and recomputed on 2026-10-21.
